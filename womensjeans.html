Women's Jeans

When you want women's jeans that look fabulous, flatter your figure and don't break the bank, you've gotta go to Old Navy. We're famous for our jeans not just for women but for the whole family. Join the millions of fans who have discovered our bestselling women's jeans – Rockstar Super Skinny Jeans. Available in high-waisted, mid-rise or low-rise styles, these women's skinny jeans are universally flattering on everybody and every body, whether you're slim, curvy, straight or plus-size.

Skinny jeans not your thing? We also have a large selection of straight fit jeans, boyfriend fit jeans, boot-cut jeans and flare-leg jeans for women. Old Navy women's jeans are comfortable, fashionable, durable and affordable. Let's talk comfort. Our women's jeans use innovative technology to keep you feeling as great as you look.
